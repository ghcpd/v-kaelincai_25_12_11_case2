❌ Dijkstra-only routing fails silently on **negative-weight graphs** → returns suboptimal paths  
❌ Example: Returns A→B (cost 5) instead of A→C→D→F→B (cost 1)  
❌ No validation, no idempotency, no timeout, no circuit-breaker, no audit trail  
❌ 2 tests, ~40% code coverage

✅ **Comprehensive testing**: 22 tests, >95% coverage, all patterns tested
